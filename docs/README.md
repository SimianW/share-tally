## Maintaining the documents

Keep definitions in the glossary. Record decision rationale in ADRs when the choice is costly to reverse and needs explanation. Keep requirements and work tracking in GitHub Issues.

The former spec draft now links to the published issue. Update requirements and unanswered design questions on that issue; do not maintain a duplicate local specification or turn proposals into accepted ADRs.

The archive preserves history, including obsolete recommendations. Consult it for the conversation's background, not current requirements.
