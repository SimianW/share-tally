# Item costs derive from printed price and the receipt summary

Per-item tax and adjustment fields overlapped with receipt-wide allocations, leaving initiators unsure which number to correct. The owner chose one derivation: an item's cost is its printed price minus its own discount, plus its proportional share of the receipt-wide discount, tax (taxable items only, when prices exclude tax) and other adjustments. Initiators no longer enter per-item tax or adjustments; directly setting an item's cost is the only manual override, and it is labelled as such wherever the derivation is shown. Existing drafts with per-item tax or adjustments keep their current item costs as manual overrides, so no amount changes silently.

Initiation stores the receipt summary with the bill (subtotal, receipt-wide discount, tax, other adjustments, total, whether prices include tax) and freezes the tax rate as receipt tax divided by the taxable amount. Participants see each item's derivation, the receipt summary and the photo, so they can check a price before claiming it. When the initiator corrects an item's printed price, discount or taxability after initiation, only that item is recalculated at the frozen rate; other items and their confirmations are unchanged, as ADR-0007 requires. Re-allocating receipt-wide amounts across all items was rejected because it would silently change, and invalidate, items nobody edited. Any resulting difference from the paid total is borne by the initiator under ADR-0008.
