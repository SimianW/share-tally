# Members leave only when settled and uninvolved in unfinished activity

The project owner chose to let a member leave a group, and the Group owner remove another member, only when that member's net balance in the group is exactly zero, no incomplete bill they initiated or take part in remains, and no pending repayment record they sent or must decide remains (#214). A zero balance alone is not enough: an incomplete bill could still give them a share, and a pending repayment could still change their balance, after they could no longer see or act on either. Only the departing member's own activity counts, so the group is never frozen while other members finish theirs. Eligibility uses the net balance from the group ledger, never individual shares, pairwise direct debts, other groups' balances or a rounding tolerance. Departure never cancels bills, removes participants or decides repayments for anyone; the existing bill and repayment rules resolve each blocker.

Management passes through a single transferable Group owner role, initialized from each group's creator. An owner leaves by choosing another current member, who becomes owner immediately with no acceptance step and whatever their own balance; the transfer and the departure commit together or not at all. A sole remaining owner deletes the cleared group under [ADR-0013](0013-soft-delete-cleared-groups.md) instead, so an active group always has exactly one current owner.

Departure deletes the membership row, so every existing membership check ends the former member's access to the group's bills, ledger, repayments, receipts, note photos, drafts and event streams. Bills, shares, item claims and repayment records keep the former member's stable identity, and group ledger reads name them as former members. Their uninitiated drafts in that group are deleted with their receipt photos, raw receipt evidence and note photos, because nobody could open them any more. Departure is not a ban: a valid invitation link lets them rejoin as an ordinary member with their history, but without their deleted drafts or a former ownership.

The eligibility check, ownership transfer, draft purge and membership change run in one transaction holding the group row lock that joins, deletion and every bill, draft and repayment write take first. A write committing first is seen by the departure check; a write queued behind a departure then finds the departed member is no longer a member.
