# Small bill differences belong to the initiator

Independently rounded participant amounts can differ from the purchase total even when integer-cent arithmetic is exact. The owner chose to allow completion after every participant confirms when the absolute difference is at most CAD 0.05 and assigning that difference to the initiator leaves their effective cost nonnegative, replacing the earlier exact-equality requirement.

Keep submitted shares unchanged and record the difference as a separate initiator adjustment used in financial balances. Show the original difference and adjustment even after completion, and explain the adjustment when creating a bill; this permits small rounding differences while keeping group balances consistent without silently rewriting confirmed shares.
