# Complete item bills by confirmed allocation

The owner chose separate completion rules for manual and item-based bills. Manual bills retain ADR-0004's CAD 0.05 tolerance. Item-based bills complete when all items are fully claimed and confirmed and every participant has responded, without a CAD 0.05 difference gate. Fractions are exact, each participant's summed cost is rounded to cents, and the difference from the actual paid total is recorded separately as an initiator adjustment without changing submitted shares.

This allows an item bill to complete even when independently rounded shares or reviewed item costs differ from the paid total by more than CAD 0.05. The initiator bears that difference, which must be visible during review and after completion. Preserve the existing nonnegative effective initiator-cost invariant: if the adjustment would make that cost negative, keep the bill incomplete until the initiator corrects the prices or paid total and participants provide the required confirmations. This prevents reimbursement exceeding actual expenditure without restoring the manual-mode tolerance gate.
