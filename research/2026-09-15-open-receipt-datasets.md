## Recommendation

Use CORD v2 first. It is the only option in this short list where the publisher's dataset license is explicit and the public release has image-level OCR plus grouped, fine-grained line-item fields. CORD's Indonesian receipts will not match Costco's English layouts, so use it to prototype the extraction shape and evaluation code, then validate on the project's SROIE samples or newly labeled receipts.

Receipt header fields are a different task from line-item extraction. SROIE and [UIT-MLReceipts](https://doi.org/10.21553/rev-jec.330) mainly label merchant/store, address, date/time, and total. Those datasets can test header OCR/KIE, but their header labels do not directly supervise `item name + quantity + price` extraction. WildReceipt has item-related regions and is closer to the need, while CORD and ReceiptSense provide the most direct item-level supervision. UIT-MLReceipts has 2,147 Vietnamese receipt images and COCO boxes, but its authors state that it is for non-commercial research and list only the four header classes.

## Practical license rule

Keep three records for any data we use: the image/data license, the annotation license, and the license for loader or training code. A GitHub MIT or Apache badge commonly covers the code only. "Download" or "public" also does not mean commercial reuse. For CORD, retain CC BY attribution. For WildReceipt, SROIE, ReceiptSense, and UIT-MLReceipts, obtain a rights answer from the dataset authors before bundling images, redistributing samples, or training a commercial service.
