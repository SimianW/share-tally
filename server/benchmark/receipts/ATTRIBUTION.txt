SHARETALLY LABELLED RECEIPT BENCHMARK — ATTRIBUTION AND LICENCES

This directory is a collection of third-party receipt images, adapted by
irreversible privacy redaction and metadata removal, with image-checked
transcriptions. It does not have one blanket image licence. Each source
manifest.json is the authoritative per-image attribution and licence record.
Retain that record and this notice when redistributing any selected images.
The original-image URL and SHA-256 identify the source; the committed-image
SHA-256 identifies the privacy-redacted adaptation.

OWNER SLOTS
owner-slots/manifest.json contains placeholders only. No owner's photo is
included, and no ownership, consent or redistribution licence is inferred.
The owner must supply these explicitly before adding images in issue #50.
